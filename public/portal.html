          Transparent Public Procedure        

          How JanSakshi AI Works / పౌర పరిష్కార విధానం        

          An automated, accountable 3-step bridge connecting citizens directly with ward-level executive municipal officers without middle agents.        

01mic

Register Grievance

దశ 1: ఫిర్యాదు నమోదు

Submit through Telugu voice note, text, or geotagged photo. JanSakshi AI automatically translates, transcribes, categorizes the grievance, and auto-detects the exact Ward and Landmark.

• AI Automatic Telugu NLP Parser
• Geotagged coordinate verification
• Instant 10-Digit SMS acknowledgement
Input Time: Under 60 Seconds
02assignment_turned_in

Action & Field Inspection

దశ 2: క్షేత్ర స్థాయి తనిఖీ

Directly assigned to GVMC/Ward executive officer with a strict statutory SLA countdown timer. Departmental teams inspect and dispatch field rectification crews.

• Time-bound Statutory SLA enforcement
• Auto-escalation if delayed > 48 hours
• Direct SMS alerts with Officer Phone Number
SLA Limit: 24 to 72 Hours
03verified_user

Citizen Verification & Sign-Off

దశ 3: పౌరుల నిర్ధారణ

Officer uploads mandatory 'Before & After' photographic evidence. The complaint is strictly NOT closed until the citizen receives an OTP and confirms genuine satisfaction.

• Mandatory photo verification
• Citizen OTP Closure confirmation
• Instant Re-open privilege if dissatisfied
Zero Bogus Closures Guaranteed

              Frequently Asked Questions (తరచుగా అడిగే ప్రశ్నలు)            

              Guidance for first-time digital users, senior citizens, and rural ward residents.            

1. Can I submit a complaint in Telugu without typing? (టైప్ చేయకుండా తెలుగులో ఫిర్యాదు చేయవచ్చా?) check_circle

                  Yes. JanSakshi AI allows you to record a voice note in Telugu or English. Our speech recognition automatically translates and converts your audio into an official departmental complaint with ward details.                

2. How do I know if my issue is resolved genuinely? (సమస్య పరిష్కరించబడిందో లేదో ఎలా నిర్ధారించుకోవాలి?) check_circle

                  Ward officers are required to upload before-and-after photographic evidence. You will receive an SMS with an approval OTP. The complaint will remain 'Under Review' until you confirm satisfaction.                

3. What if an officer ignores the grievance? (సమస్యను అధికారి నిర్లక్ష్యం చేస్తే ఏమి జరుగుతుంది?) check_circle

                  Every civic category has a strict SLA timer (e.g. 24 hours for streetlights, 48 hours for garbage). If unaddressed, the system auto-escalates the file directly to the Zonal Commissioner and alerts vigilance.                

4. Is JanSakshi AI free of charge for all citizens? (ఈ సేవ పూర్తిగా ఉచితమా?) check_circle

                  Yes. JanSakshi AI is a 100% free municipal service. No citizen fees or service charges are applicable at any stage of registration or grievance redressal.
